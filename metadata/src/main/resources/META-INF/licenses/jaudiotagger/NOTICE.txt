The bundled library is a read-only subset. Two DFF reader classes are modified:
org.jaudiotagger.audio.dff.BaseChunk and DffFileReader.
Changes: bounded chunk traversal, odd-byte padding, correct DSD form-type length,
and skipping unknown/ID3 payloads. No audio decoding algorithms are changed.
The modified source is included in metadata/src/main/java/org/jaudiotagger/audio/dff
in this project; source headers and original attribution are retained.
